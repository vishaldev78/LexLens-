==================================================
PRODUCT DIRECTION
==================================================

LexLens should NOT be positioned as:

"Upload a legal notice and ChatGPT explains it."

That is too easy to replicate with a general AI model.

LexLens should become:

"An evidence-backed legal notice action engine that converts an unstructured legal notice into verified facts, missing information, a legal timeline, deadline tracking, prioritized actions, and a lawyer-ready case brief."

Core product promise:

FROM:
Long legal notice / PDF / image

TO:
1. Important facts
2. Missing information
3. Verified legal provisions
4. Timeline
5. Deadline
6. What to do now
7. Evidence checklist
8. Lawyer-ready brief

The application must prioritize ACTION over generic explanation.

FEATURE 9 — REMOVE THE CURRENT INCORRECT 30-DAY RIGHT
==================================================

The current analysis has a statement similar to:

"Right to dispute the debt within 30 days of receiving this notice."

This MUST NOT be generated as a Section 138 statutory right.

The 30-day period associated with Section 138 concerns the payee/holder issuing the statutory demand notice after receiving information about cheque dishonour.

The recipient's statutory payment period is 15 days from receipt of the notice, subject to the statutory requirements.

Therefore:

REMOVE:
"Right to dispute the debt within 30 days."

Also do NOT automatically generate:

"Right to request creditor details under Section 138"

unless the legal source explicitly supports that exact proposition.

If the user disputes the claim, LexLens may recommend obtaining legal advice and preparing an appropriate written response, but do not falsely attribute a generic dispute right to Section 138.
