   465→FEATURE 9 — REMOVE THE CURRENT INCORRECT 30-DAY RIGHT
   466→==================================================
   467→
   468→The current analysis has a statement similar to:
   469→
   470→"Right to dispute the debt within 30 days of receiving this notice."
   471→
   472→This MUST NOT be generated as a Section 138 statutory right.
   473→
   474→The 30-day period associated with Section 138 concerns the payee/holder issuing the statutory demand notice after receiving information about cheque dishonour.
   475→
   476→The recipient's statutory payment period is 15 days from receipt of the notice, subject to the statutory requirements.
   477→
   478→Therefore:
   479→
   480→REMOVE:
   481→"Right to dispute the debt within 30 days."
   482→
   483→Also do NOT automatically generate:
   484→
   485→"Right to request creditor details under Section 138"
   486→
   487→unless the legal source explicitly supports that exact proposition.
   488→
   489→If the user disputes the claim, LexLens may recommend obtaining legal advice and preparing an appropriate written response, but do not falsely attribute a generic dispute right to Section 138.
